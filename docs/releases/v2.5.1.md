# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.5.1

**Released 13 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

Reference data first: the TL-200M example now charts the jib the manufacturer
specifies, and its specification sheet ships beside the workbook so every figure
can be checked against the source. The rest of the release packs the printed
plan's head into the top third of its first page.

## Highlights

- **The TL-200M example matches its specification sheet.** The sample workbook
  charts its jib at the manufacturer's **8.0 m** (it said 7.2 m). A copied
  example is reference data, so a figure in it matters more than any layout
  change here.

- **The first page of the plan is packed into its top third.** The plan details
  and the crane figures are laid out two pairs wide, and the capacity verdict
  (`WITHIN LIMIT` / `CAUTION` / `WARNING`) is its own coloured band directly
  under the figures it judges, before the working that produced them.

- **The manufacturer's sheet ships with the workbook.** The TL-200M
  specification PDF now sits in `docs/examples/` beside the workbook it
  transcribes.

## Compatibility and upgrade notes

- **Linux.** The AppImage needs glibc 2.35 or newer: Ubuntu 22.04+, Fedora 36+,
  Arch and anything at least as new as those. An installed AppImage updates in
  place with `appimageupdatetool` and this release's `.zsync` sidecar.
- **Windows.** Windows 10 or 11, x64, with the WebView2 runtime (the installer
  ships the bootstrapper). Installing over 2.5.0 keeps nothing to migrate: the
  app stores no plan between launches by design.
- **Printing.** Leave the print dialog's margin selector on **Default**. The
  page sets a one-inch A4 margin of its own, but *Minimum* and *None* override
  any document's margin.
