# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.9.0

**Released 16 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

The Crane tab does less typing and says one thing: the workbook names the
machine, and the load chart is the only capacity check.

## Highlights

- **The workbook names the crane.** A title line above the chart
  (`Tadano TL200M (20 ton)`) now fills **Crane name** and **Crane rated
  capacity** when the chart is imported, so neither has to be typed and the
  plan carries the machine's own name. A workbook without that line leaves
  whatever was typed alone.

- **One capacity check.** The booked-rating check is withdrawn: the load chart
  is the rating that applies at the boom and radius in use, and a second test
  against the crane's plate rating answered a different question. The "Booked
  rating check" row is gone from the Crane tab, and the plan's usage band is the
  chart's alone. The rated capacity is still printed for the record.

- **The field is named for what it holds.** The Crane tab now reads **Crane
  name** where it read "Crane".

## Notes

- No calculation, chart reading, radius rule or report layout changed. A
  radius outside the chart is still refused with the range the chart rates.
- The example workbooks are corrected and carry their manufacturers'
  specification sheets in `docs/examples/`: the TL-200M is a 20 t crane and the
  TR350M-1 a 35 t crane.
