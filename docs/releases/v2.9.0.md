# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.9.0

**Released 16 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

The Crane tab does less typing and says one thing: the workbook names the
machine, and the load chart is the only capacity check.

## Highlights

- **The workbook names the crane.** A title line above the chart
  (`Tadano TL200M (20 ton)`) now fills **Crane name** and **Crane rated
  capacity** when the chart is imported, so neither has to be typed and the
  plan carries the machine's own name. A workbook without that line leaves
  whatever was typed alone.

- **One capacity check.** The booked-rating check is withdrawn: the load chart
  is the rating that applies at the boom and radius in use, and a second test
  against the crane's plate rating answered a different question. The "Booked
  rating check" row is gone from the Crane tab, and the plan's usage band is the
  chart's alone. The rated capacity is still printed for the record.

- **The field is named for what it holds.** The Crane tab now reads **Crane
  name** where it read "Crane".

- **The rigging factors read two to a row.** Every factor the arrangement uses
  — the angle factor, the base reeve factor, a choke's reduction and combined
  factor, the basket's longitudinal/transverse/combined factors, and a nested
  hitch's whole chain — is laid out two to a row like the crane's required
  capacity and usage, so the section is half as tall and each formula keeps its
  label.

- **Page margins you choose.** **Margins** beside *Refresh* in the report view
  opens four fields in millimetres (5–50, with 1 inch, 20 mm, 0.5 inch and 10 mm
  presets) and shows the text area each choice leaves on A4. The page view,
  *Save as PDF* and the exported HTML all use the same margin, and the footer
  keeps its place inside the bottom one. One inch stays the default.

- **A compact Note section.** The choke notes and the tag-rating rule now sit
  side by side in one row under one-line headings, and the divider between any
  two columns follows the content: true centre when both sides fit, wider for
  whichever side needs it. The choker note's "(1) Percent of sling rated
  capacity…" paragraph is no longer printed, and its reduction table is two
  tables side by side: 120° to 60° first, the rest beside it.

- **A two-leg choke no longer claims to hang vertical.** The note about a
  vertical leg was printed for every choke while the angle factor beside it was
  below 1.0. It now appears only for the single-leg chokes, which do stand
  vertical.

## Notes

- No calculation, chart reading, radius rule or report layout changed. A
  radius outside the chart is still refused with the range the chart rates.
- The example workbooks are corrected and carry their manufacturers'
  specification sheets in `docs/examples/`: the TL-200M is a 20 t crane and the
  TR350M-1 a 35 t crane.
