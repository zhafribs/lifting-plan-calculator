# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.17.13

**Released 22 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage, deb, rpm, Arch, Flatpak) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

Every sling arrangement now prints its long workings on two lines, with the
continuation under the first equals. No calculation changed.

## Changed

- **Two-line workings in every arrangement.** Uniform, nested, bridle,
  nonuniform and tandem workings, plus the rigging factors and the capacity
  section, group a long formula into two lines. The split is balanced so the
  wider line stays short.
- **The continuation lines up.** The second line starts under the first equals,
  so it cannot spill past its column.
- **Space is kept.** A two-line block stays in its half-column unless its lines
  are genuinely wide; only then does it take the full row.

## Notes

- No arithmetic, verdict or limit changed. A formula too wide for the full row
  still keeps one step per line rather than shrinking the page.
- The Windows installer still carries the WebView2 bootstrapper.
