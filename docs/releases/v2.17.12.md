# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.17.12

**Released 22 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage, deb, rpm, Arch, Flatpak) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

The plan header is one box: the diagram, its workings and the line lists now
read together, with the workings taking the room they need. No calculation
changed.

## Changed

- **One box for the plan header.** The working range diagram and the Load Items
  and Lifting Tackle lists sit inside the Plan details border.
- **The diagram takes the left, the workings the right.** At the default 50%
  the diagram leaves room, so Required capacity, Usage and the line lists stack
  on its right. Each working stays at most two lines.
- **A readable fallback.** At 100% — or whenever the diagram leaves no room —
  the workings drop under it: Required capacity and Usage side by side, two
  lines each, with the lists below.
- **The list heading matches.** Load items and lifting tackle now uses the same
  bold label as the two workings.

## Notes

- No arithmetic, verdict or limit changed. The Usage working is split at its
  equals sign when space is tight; the numbers and words are the same.
- The Windows installer still carries the WebView2 bootstrapper.
