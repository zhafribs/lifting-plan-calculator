# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.17.8

**Released 21 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage, deb, rpm, Arch, Flatpak) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

The plan now lists the lines that built it, its geometry reads two workings to
a row, the square pad is one centred rule, and the working range diagram can be
scaled in the report. No calculation changed.

## Added

- **The plan lists the load and tackle lines.** Two small lists side by side —
  description and subtotal — with the load items on the left and the lifting
  tackle on the right. They sit under the Required capacity / Usage working, or
  under the working range diagram when that is printed.
- **The working range diagram has a scale.** The Report sections card carries a
  50 / 75 / 100% control beside the diagram tick; the chosen width shows in the
  preview, the saved HTML and the PDF.

## Changed

- **Section 4 reads two workings to a row.** Every sling arrangement — direct,
  nested, round choke and rectangular choke — uses the report's two-column
  working grid. No formula was shortened; the fixture report dropped from seven
  pages to five.
- **The square pad is one centred rule.** The bold `Minimum square pad` and
  `Side = √A = 1.396 m ≈ 1.40 m` share a line between two hairlines, and the
  duplicate “Minimum pad” sentence is gone.
- **The chart-capacity note sits beside its label** instead of under the figure.
- **The plan-details usage cell no longer repeats “of chart capacity”.** The
  caution case keeps “past the 75% target”.

## Fixed

- **Long dropdown lists scroll.** The arrangement list closed on its own wheel
  scroll; scrolling inside the list now leaves it open, while scrolling the
  page still closes it.

## Notes

- No calculation, verdict or limit changed. The removed report sentences were
  duplicates of a row above them; older printed plans keep them.
- The Windows installer still carries the WebView2 bootstrapper.

## Compatibility and upgrade notes

- AppImage and packages: glibc 2.35 floor — Ubuntu 22.04+, Fedora 36+, Arch.
- Windows: Windows 10 or 11, x64, with the WebView2 runtime; the installer
  carries its bootstrapper.
- Updating from 2.17.7 keeps every setting.
