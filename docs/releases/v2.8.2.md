## Why it is safe to take

- The full suite runs on both platforms: 203 tests, including the PDF stamping
  tests that use a real printed plan as a fixture.
- The export probe prints and stamps a real PDF on Linux and on Windows through
  WebView2, including the save locations and the two refusals.
- The workbook reader keeps its example charts: the Excel tests parse the two
  charts in `docs/examples/`.

`webview2-com` and `windows-core` are deliberately *not* updated here: they are
version-coupled to tauri's own WebView2 bindings, and moving them on their own
breaks the Windows printer. They move when tauri moves.

## Compatibility and upgrade notes

- glibc 2.35 floor — Ubuntu 22.04+, Fedora 36+, Arch.
- No plan is stored between launches, so there is nothing to migrate.
- The document format, the report layout and the printed footer are unchanged.
