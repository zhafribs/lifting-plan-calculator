# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.17.9

**Released 21 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage, deb, rpm, Arch, Flatpak) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

A sheet of load lines is now sized by its heaviest lift, rigging counts with the
lift it rides with, and the report is assembled from sections you can switch on
and off. No arithmetic inside a lift changed.

## Highlights

- **Load lines are grouped into lifts.** Every load line carries a Lift field.
  Lines under one name are lifted together and add up; different names are
  alternatives, and the overall weight comes from the **heaviest lift**. A
  concrete pour and its bucket can share one lift while steel and scaffold stay
  separate. The summary names the heaviest lift and the printed plan says which
  lift sets the overall weight.
- **Tackle lines carry a lift too.** Rigging counts with the lift it rides
  with: overall weight is the heaviest lift's load plus **that lift's tackle**.
  Tackle tagged with a lift that is not in the plan is treated as shared and
  counted on every lift rather than dropped.
- **The plan is built from sections.** Plan details now sits above Crane in the
  Report sections card, and Load Items and Lifting Tackle sits between the
  working range diagram and the outrigger. A new plan opens with Plan details,
  Crane, Working Range Diagram, Load Items and Lifting Tackle, and Outrigger Pad
  Size ticked. Unticking everything leaves one blank page rather than a print
  error.

## Added

- **The Overall Weight tab has an Actions card.** Load example fills a 1,500 kg
  steel plate and an 80 kg 2-leg chain sling; Show report opens the plan with
  the crane section; Clear returns both lists to one blank line.

## Changed

- **The description and measured-by controls are boxed.** In both the load and
  tackle cards the description field and the measured-by picker carry the same
  border as the Lift field. The measured value is the same height as the
  quantity box beside it.
- **The diagram scale is typed, not picked.** The Report sections card takes a
  number between 25 and 100, normalised when focus leaves. Only the width is
  set; the height follows, so the diagram keeps its own ratio.

## Fixed

- **Every uniform arrangement prints at 100%.** A formula wider than its
  half-column made WebKit shrink the whole printed page. Long formulas now
  break at their equals signs and a block still too wide takes the full row.
- **Printing the live preview no longer scales the plan.** The frame is reset
  to 100% for the print job and re-fitted afterwards, so Print and Save as PDF
  carry the same scale.
- **The working range diagram scales both ways.** Its height now follows the
  width, so the capture keeps its own ratio.

## Notes

- The arithmetic for a lift is unchanged; the plan is now sized by the heaviest
  lift instead of the sum of every line. Lift names are matched exactly.
- The Windows installer still carries the WebView2 bootstrapper.

## Compatibility and upgrade notes

- AppImage and packages: glibc 2.35 floor — Ubuntu 22.04+, Fedora 36+, Arch.
- Windows: Windows 10 or 11, x64, with the WebView2 runtime; the installer
  carries its bootstrapper.
- Updating from 2.17.8 keeps every setting. Lift names are new, so existing
  offers do not carry one; a line without a lift reads as Lift 1.
