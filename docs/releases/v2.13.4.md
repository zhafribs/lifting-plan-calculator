# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.13.4

**Released 18 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

Smaller outrigger report and form fixes.

## Changed

- **The printed bearing-area formula is one line.** The report now prints
  `A = 0.65 × (CM + L) / V = ... = ...` as one equation instead of two.
- **The labels mark the variable.** `Design (V)`, `Design load (P)` and
  `Required pad area (A)` replace the unmarked labels.
- **The pad card separates its extra fields.** The **Rectangular pad** width and
  the **Timber 45° rule** foot dimensions sit under their own labelled sections.
  The **Use crane tab** button sits under **Crane mass (CM)**, and **Use overall
  weight** sits under **Lift load (L)**.

## Notes

- The bearing-area formula, ground table, minimum-pad result and thickness
  guidance are unchanged.
- The outrigger report keeps the two-column layout: design load beside bearing
  area, pad thickness beside ground.
- The Linux AppImage self-update and the GitHub feed fallback are unchanged.
- Windows and the rest of the app are unchanged.

## Compatibility and upgrade notes

- glibc 2.35 floor — Ubuntu 22.04+, Fedora 36+, Arch.
- Windows needs the WebView2 runtime, as before; the installers remain unsigned,
  so SmartScreen shows "Unknown publisher".
- No plan is stored between launches, so there is nothing to migrate.

**Scope** — a calculation aid for a competent person, not a certified lift plan or an approval. [Scope of use](https://github.com/zhafribs/lifting-plan-calculator#scope-of-use)
