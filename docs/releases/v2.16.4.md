# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.16.4

**Released 19 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage, deb, rpm, Arch, Flatpak) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

A wallpaper-themed Hyprland desktop is now recognised as it is: Noctalia themes
every application from the wallpaper, and the app joins them without a setting.

## Fixed

- **Noctalia is read directly.** The app looks at the palette Noctalia writes for
  the terminal (`~/.config/kitty/themes/noctalia.conf`) and at Noctalia's own
  light/dark mode (`~/.config/noctalia/config.toml`). Nothing to configure: the
  accent and the tinted surfaces follow the wallpaper, and they follow the
  desktop's scheme even when GTK does not advertise it.

- **Generated GTK stylesheets are read too.** If `~/.config/gtk-3.0/gtk.css` (or
  the GTK 4 one) declares `@define-color accent_color`, that colour is used.
  This also covers matugen-style setups that template GTK.

## Notes

- The order is: pywal or wallust palette, Noctalia's terminal theme, the
  generated `gtk.css`, the desktop accent (GNOME/KDE), then the app's own blue.
- Changing the wallpaper is picked up when you return to the app window; the
  choice is re-read then, not polled.
- Nothing changed in the calculation, the report or the printed diagram.
