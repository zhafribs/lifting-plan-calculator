# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.5.2

**Released 13 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

The printed plan's head is now a summary card instead of a bordered grid, and the
booked-rating check explains itself on the page. No calculation changed.

## Highlights

- **The plan's head reads as a summary, not a form.** The title and crane sit
  together, the capacity verdict is a pill beside the crane under a **Capacity
  check** label, the plan details are a quiet strip, and the six deciding figures
  are cards: gross load, chart capacity, usage, required capacity at 75%, booked
  rating, and boom / radius. The chart source is a caption under them.

- **The booked rating explains itself.** The card says whether the crane's plate
  rating clears what the lift needs at 75% — "passes — 3,600.00 kg needed at 75%"
  or "below — 3,600.00 kg needed at 75%" — and one line under the card says what
  the check is for: the chart answers "here", the booked rating answers "at all".

- **The verdict names the capacity it judges.** It now reads "WITHIN LIMIT —
  16.88% of chart capacity at working radius", so it cannot be mistaken for the
  crane's booked capacity.

## Changed

- The printed plan's head is a summary card: title and crane, a capacity-check
  pill, plan details as a five-column strip, six figure cards, the verdict
  sentence, and the chart source as a caption. The top of the sheet stays inside
  its first third.
- The sign-off labels are shorter: "Reference", "Issued", "Prepared by",
  "Checked by", "Approved by".
- The usage percentage is printed to two decimals in both the card and the
  working, so the same figure appears once.
- The printed plan's foot states its scope: a calculation aid only, not a
  certification, with every figure to be checked before the lift.
