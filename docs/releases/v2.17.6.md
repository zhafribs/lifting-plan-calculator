# Lifting Plan Calculator 2.17.6

**Released 20 November 2026** · Linux (AppImage, deb, rpm, Arch, Flatpak) · Windows 10/11 (x64)

A fixed-weight load line keeps its dropdown between the quantity and the weight
box when the navigation is expanded in a small window. No calculation changed.

## Fixed

- **The fixed-weight row no longer overlaps in a narrow card.** With the rail
  expanded beside a small window, the card shrank below the width the two-line
  row needs. The 62px quantity slid under the 156px dropdown, which clipped
  "Fixed" to "ed". Below a 485px card the quantity now has its own column and
  the dropdown takes the space left between it and the weight box, so the row
  stays on two lines and nothing overlaps.
- Per-metre and per-volume rows keep the layout from 2.17.4, and the app-drawn
  dropdowns from 2.17.5 are unchanged.

## Notes

- No calculation, report content or PDF layout changed.

## Compatibility and upgrade notes

- AppImage and packages: glibc 2.35 floor — Ubuntu 22.04+, Fedora 36+, Arch.
- Windows: Windows 10 or 11, x64, with the WebView2 runtime; the installer
  carries its bootstrapper.
- Updating from 2.17.5 keeps every setting.
